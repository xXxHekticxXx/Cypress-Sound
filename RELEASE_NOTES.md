Download **Cypress-Sound-Setup.exe** below and run it.

- Switch outputs with one click, or Ctrl+Alt+1-9 from anywhere (even in games).
- Each output keeps its own EQ and loads it by itself when you switch.
- 10-band EQ, parametric EQ, headphone correction for 6,000+ models (AutoEq), presets, bass boost, night mode, mono, balance.
- Per-app volume and per-app output from the tray icon.
- Auto profiles: a preset (and output) switches on while a game or player is running.
- Mic EQ for calls and streaming.
